原有座位、床位、储物空间和进入方式。[*]每款都有“水箱”和“副水箱”两个净水箱位，不增加专用武器柜；两个水箱分别命名，并可在液体转移界面单独选择。[/list][h1]可安装设备[/h1][list][*][b]床铺与储物[/b]：支持行军床、床垫、睡袋、小型木柜和原版直柜台；Van 与 StepVan 另有绿色武器箱位。[*][b]家电[/b]：支持微波炉、小冰箱和三种电视；电视可从前排或后排使用频道、音量和媒体，KI5 房车会显示正确的冰箱图标。[*][b]饮水与供电[/b]：饮水机水桶可与水箱或随身容器转移液体；车载电源负责冰箱、微波炉、电视和进水净化。[/list][h1]净水系统[/h1][list][*]StepVan 有 1 个净水箱；KI5 10%。[*]维修界面同时显示瓦斯剩余量和瓦斯桶本身的耐久。[*]安装有剩余瓦斯的瓦斯桶时，站在车旁即可给随身未充满的喷灯补充瓦斯。[/list][h1]沙盒选项[/h1][list][*]冰箱、微波炉、电视和净水可分别设置耗电量；冰箱持续耗电，其他设备按使用或进水量耗电。[/list][h1]原作者与原模组[/h1][list][*]原作者：KI5[*]房车原模组：[url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3670064951]Campers![/url][*]前置：[url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3171167894]damnlib[/url][/list][h1]两个版本与兼容[/h1][list][*][b]原版车辆版 42.20、单人、多人和专用服务器。[*]长期存档增删车辆模组前请先备份。[/list][hr][h1]Mobile Living[/h1][quote]Turn vanilla vehicles and KI5 campers into configurable mobile living spaces with beds, storage, appliances, water equipment, tanks, and vehicle power.[/quote][h1]Vanilla Vehicles[/h1][list][*][b]SUV / PickUpVan[/b]: keeps every original seat and adds 1 independent living space.[*][b]Van[/b]: keeps 2 front seats and adds 3 independent living spaces with no water tank.[*][b]StepVan[/b]: keeps 2 front seats and adds 5 independent living spaces plus 1 clean-water tank position.[*]Passenger Vans, wrecks, and burnt vehicles are excluded.[/list][h1]KI5 Campers[/h1][list][*][b]1987 Scamp 13[/b]: adds 2 independent living spaces.[*][b]1987 Scamp 16[/b]: adds 3 independent living spaces.[*][b]1961 Bambi 16[/b]: adds 3 independent living spaces.[*][b]1954 Flying Cloud 22[/b]: adds 4 independent living spaces.[*]Original KI5 seats, beds, storage, and entry behavior are preserved.[*]Each camper gains a Water Tank and an Auxiliary Water Tank, with no dedicated weapon locker. The tanks have distinct names and separate fluid-transfer selections.[/list][h1]Installable Equipment[/h1][list][*][b]Beds and storage[/b]: supports cots, mattresses, sleeping bags, small cabinets, and straight vanilla counters; Vans and StepVans also have a green weapon-box position.[*][b]Appliances[/b]: supports microwaves, mini fridges, and all three vanilla TVs. Televisions open from front or rear seats with channel, volume, and media controls; KI5 campers display the correct refrigerator icon.[*][b]Water and power[/b]: water-dispenser bottles transfer liquid with tanks or carried containers; vehicle power runs refrigeration, microwaves, televisions, and incoming-water purification.[/list][h1]Clean-Water System[/h1][list][*]StepVans have 1 clean-water tank; KI5 campers have a main and auxiliary tank.[*]Tank positions accept vanilla mechanic-type-2 fuel tanks. Fill from usable water sources and transfer liquid among tanks, water-dispenser bottles, and carried containers; purification consumes vehicle power.[*]Water tanks must be empty before removal; the mechanics window shows remaining water and tank condition.[/list][h1]KI5 Original-Feature Fixes and Additions[/h1][list][*]KI5's original battery powers all living equipment; no second battery is needed.[*]Propane tanks display their correct item condition instead of showing an intact tank as 10%.[*]The mechanics window shows both remaining propane and propane-tank durability.[*]With propane remaining in an installed tank, stand beside the camper to refill an incomplete carried blowtorch.[/list][h1]Sandbox Options[/h1][list][*]Refrigeration, microwave use, television use, and water purification have separate power settings; fridges drain continuously, while the others scale with use or incoming water.[/list][h1]Original Author and Mods[/h1][list][*]Original author: KI5[*]Original camper mod: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3670064951]Campers![/url][*]Dependency: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3171167894]damnlib[/url][/list][h1]Two Versions and Compatibility[/h1][list][*][b]Vanilla Version RC3.6[/b]: enable [b]VehicleLivingSlots[/b] only; no dependency is required.[*][b]Vanilla + KI5 Version RC3.6[/b]: enable both [b]VehicleLivingSlots[/b] and [b]VehicleLivingSlotsKI5Campers[/b], with the original mod and dependency listed above.[*]Both versions support Project Zomboid Build 42.20, singleplayer, multiplayer, and dedicated servers.[*]Back up long-running worlds before adding or removing vehicle mods.[/list]
