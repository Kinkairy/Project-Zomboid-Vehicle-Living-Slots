title=Mobile Living
description=[h1]房车生活[/h1]
description=把受支持的车辆改装成移动生活空间，安装床铺、储物柜、家电和车顶装备。支持 B42.20 单人、多人及专用服务器。
description=[h2]功能特色[/h2]
description=[list][*]车内生活：安装床铺、柜台、储物柜、小冰箱、微波炉和电视，在车内休息、收纳和使用设备。
description=[*]饮水与供电：车载电源支持生活设备；配备水箱的车型支持储水、净化和液体转移。耗电可通过沙盒选项调整。
description=[*]车顶装备：为受支持的原版车型安装行李架、发电机、储物箱、帐篷、油罐、瓦斯罐及备胎。
description=[*]车顶射灯：独立安装，使用车辆主电瓶供电，随原车大灯开关。
description=[*]车辆防护：焊接安装车窗护甲和前后防撞杠，具有独立耐久与破损外观；拆毁可回收部分材料。[/list]
description=[h2]支持范围[/h2]
description=[list][*]原版 StepVan、Van、SUV、PickUpVan 及指定变种，包括邮政版。客运 Van 可安装车顶装备，不增加车内生活空间。
description=[*]不改装 PickUpTruck、指定警用／救护／广播等特殊车型，以及损毁、烧毁车辆。
description=[*]可选 KI5 Campers! 适配，为四款受支持房车增加生活设备位与净水箱，同时保留原有座位、床位和储物功能。车顶改装及新增护甲限受支持的原版车型。[/list]
description=[h2]启用方式[/h2]
description=[list][*]原版车辆：启用 VehicleLivingSlots，无需其他 Mod。
description=[*]KI5 房车：额外启用 VehicleLivingSlotsKI5Campers，并安装 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3670064951]KI5 Campers![/url] 和 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3171167894]damnlib[/url]。
description=[*]通过车辆维修界面安装设备，所需材料、工具和技能以菜单提示为准。固定设施采用拆毁方式移除。
description=[*]多人游戏客户端与服务器须使用一致版本。版本：3.8.1。[/list]

description=[h1]Mobile Living[/h1]
description=Turn supported vehicles into mobile living spaces with beds, storage, appliances and roof equipment. Supports B42.20 single-player, multiplayer and dedicated servers.
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list][*]Living space: fit beds, counters, storage cabinets, mini fridges, microwaves and televisions for resting, storage and appliance use.
description=[*]Water and power: vehicle power supplies living equipment. Vehicles with tank slots support water storage, purification and fluid transfers. Power consumption is configurable in sandbox settings.
description=[*]Roof equipment: fit supported vanilla vehicles with racks, generators, storage boxes, packed tents, fuel cans, propane tanks and spare tyres.
description=[*]Roof spotlights: install individually; powered by the main vehicle battery and switched with the original headlights.
description=[*]Vehicle protection: weld on window armor and front/rear bumper guards with separate durability and damage visuals. Destructive removal recovers some materials.[/list]
description=[h2]Supported vehicles[/h2]
description=[list][*]Vanilla StepVan, Van, SUV, PickUpVan and selected variants, including postal vans. Passenger Vans support roof equipment without added interior living spaces.
description=[*]PickUpTrucks, specified police/ambulance/broadcast variants, wrecks and burnt vehicles are excluded.
description=[*]Optional KI5 Campers! support adds living-equipment slots and clean-water tanks to four supported campers while retaining their original seats, beds and storage. Roof upgrades and added armor are limited to supported vanilla vehicles.[/list]
description=[h2]Setup[/h2]
description=[list][*]Vanilla vehicles: enable VehicleLivingSlots. No other Mod is required.
description=[*]KI5 campers: also enable VehicleLivingSlotsKI5Campers and install [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3670064951]KI5 Campers![/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3171167894]damnlib[/url].
description=[*]Use the vehicle mechanics menu to install equipment. The menu lists required materials, tools and skills. Fixed fittings require destructive removal.
description=[*]Multiplayer clients and servers must use matching versions. Version: 3.8.1.[/list]
