Installation, power and water now use shared entry points, consolidating repeated checks. Items are assigned to suitable installation spaces according to the movability, size and placement properties of their vanilla furniture.\n\n[b]Installable in living spaces[/b]\n[list]\n[*]Mattresses, camp beds and supported sleeping bags.\n[*]Standard refrigerators, freezers, and movable single-tile stoves and ovens.\n[*]Washing machines, dryers and washer-dryer combinations.\n[*]Base cabinets, storage cabinets, shelves, wooden/metal crates, and supported tables and counters. Crafting support depends on whether the furniture has the vanilla crafting-surface property.\n[*]Antique televisions and bottled-water dispensers. Dishwashers provide only the vanilla storage/countertop capabilities that have been integrated.\n[/list]\n\n[b]Installable in overhead interior spaces[/b]\n[list]\n[*]Microwaves, mini fridges and trailer fridges.\n[*]Coffee makers, toasters, black televisions and widescreen televisions.\n[*]Wall cabinets, wall shelves, medicine cabinets, and small drawer units, crates, tables and counters that meet the size rules.\n[/list]\n\n[b]Installation restrictions and existing saves[/b]\n\nImmovable facilities and large furniture spanning multiple tiles cannot be installed, including furnaces, anvils and fixed workbenches.\n\nSmall appliances such as microwaves already installed in living spaces in existing saves remain in place and usable. Once removed, they must follow the new overhead-space rules when reinstalled.\n\nIf you encounter any compatibility issues with existing saves, please report them.\n[/quote]\n\n[h1]English[/h1]\nTurn supported vehicles into mobile living spaces with beds, storage, appliances and roof equipment. Supports B42.20 single-player, multiplayer and dedicated servers.\n\n[h2]Features[/h2]\n[list]\n[*]Rest, store items and use appliances inside. Coffee makers and toasters use the auxiliary battery; power consumption is configurable.\n[*]Vehicles with tanks support water storage, purification and transfers. Use vehicle tanks and rack-mounted propane tanks while standing nearby.\n[*]Supported roof racks accept generators, storage boxes, tents, fuel cans, propane tanks and spare tyres.\n[*]Individually installed spotlights use the main battery and follow the original headlights.\n[*]Weld on window armor and front/rear bumper guards with separate durability and damage visuals; destructive removal recovers some materials. Chassis upgrades greatly increase carrying capacity.\n[*]Operate rack-mounted generators, petrol cans, propane tanks and built-in water tanks from nearby quick menus; these menus can be disabled in Sandbox Options.\n[*]Install solar panels from materials. Deploy while parked to charge the auxiliary battery; movement retracts them. The feature has a sandbox switch.\n[/list]\n\n[h2]Supported vehicles[/h2]\n[list]\n[*]Vanilla StepVan, Van, SUV, PickUpVan and selected variants, including postal vans. Passenger Vans gain roof equipment only, without extra living space.\n[*]PickUpTrucks, specified police/ambulance/broadcast variants, wrecks and burnt vehicles are excluded.\n[*]Optional KI5 Campers! adds living slots and clean-water tanks to six campers, retaining their seats, beds and storage. Added roof upgrades and armor are limited to supported vanilla vehicles.\n[*]Optional F700 adds living slots and tanks to the bank truck, SWAT van, and School/Prison/Military Buses, retaining storage. SWAT and Bus rear seats can be converted.\n[/list]\n\n[h2]Setup[/h2]\n[list]\n[*]Vanilla vehicles: VehicleLivingSlots; no other Mod required.\n[*]KI5 campers: also enable VehicleLivingSlotsKI5Campers; install KI5 Campers! and damnlib (links above).\n[*]F700: also enable VehicleLivingSlotsKI5F700; install '87 Ford B700/F700 Trucks and damnlib (links above).\n[*]Install through vehicle mechanics; see the menu for materials, tools and skills.
